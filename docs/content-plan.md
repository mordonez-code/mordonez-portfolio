### 1 — "Running the Ferrero digital shelf on Amazon & Walmart" *(real, lead)*
- **Client/context:** Ferrero USA (Butterfinger, Crunch, Kinder, Nutella, Ferrero Rocher) via eStoreLabs. Nameable (it's on your LinkedIn) — confirm which *visuals* you may publish.
- **Her role:** Designed and optimized hero images, content carousels, and enhanced (A+) content / PDPs across multiple brands and seasonal moments; translated strict global brand guidelines into platform-specific experiences at scale.
- **No metrics available — and that's fine.** The retailer/brand owns the analytics, so there are no before/afters or conversion numbers to show. The case study is built on **judgment, not outcomes** — which is what AI/product-design hiring managers most want to see.
- **Structure (curated — not a gallery, ~8–12 images total):**
  1. **Anchor brand, full-funnel:** one iconic brand (recommend **Nutella or Ferrero Rocher**) shown end-to-end — optimized hero → content carousel → enhanced content.
  2. **One high-stakes seasonal moment:** a single peak campaign (recommend **Super Bowl**) — shows adapting the brand system under pressure. Skip the rest of the seasons.
  3. **A small range strip:** 4–6 thumbnails across Butterfinger, Crunch, Kinder, Nutella, Ferrero Rocher — proves scale + consistency.
- **How to show value without numbers:**
  - **Annotate the work** — label the design decisions on top of a PDP and explain *why* (mobile-thumbnail legibility, carousel hierarchy, brand-rules vs. platform-rules tradeoffs).
  - **Construct an illustrative baseline-vs-optimized** comparison yourself (clearly labeled as illustrative, not a measured lift).
  - **Frame the constraint:** rigid global guidelines × Amazon/Walmart × peak traffic → one consistent on-brand experience.
  - **Industry benchmark, honestly:** note that enhanced content is widely shown to lift conversion — as general context, never as *her* number.
- **Assets:** anchor-brand PDP set + one Super Bowl example + range thumbnails, all cleared for public use.
- **Optional later:** ask eStoreLabs/Agustina if anything (a number, an official before/after) is shareable — but do not block on it.
- **Priority:** 1 — marquee brand, directly proves e-commerce experience design at scale.
